## Accessibility Improvements

I tested all three pages of my portfolio using the WAVE accessibility checker.

- **Main heading contrast:** I changed the main heading text to white so it has better contrast against the dark navy background and is easier to read.
- **Navigation contrast:** I changed the navigation links to dark navy so they have stronger contrast against the gold background and are easier for users with low vision to read.
- **WAVE testing:** I tested the Home, Projects, and About pages again after making my changes and confirmed that the accessibility and contrast errors were fixed.

## Gestalt Design Principles

### Proximity

I used proximity by keeping related information close together inside sections and content cards. This makes the website easier to scan and helps users understand which information belongs together.

### Similarity

I used similarity by keeping the same colors, fonts, borders, navigation style, and content-card design across all three pages. This creates a consistent design and makes the website easier to understand and navigate.
